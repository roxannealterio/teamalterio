Holland Park West.

The headline

A $1.5 million suburb.

The median Holland Park West house sold for $1,515,000 over the twelve months to June, up 10.53%. Brisbane has turned since then, which is covered further down.

Next door, Holland Park sits at $1,579,175, up 12.80%. The two suburbs are now within about four per cent of each other on price. Holland Park West houses are selling faster, at 24 days against 27.

How fast

Twenty four days.

That is the median time a Holland Park West house takes to sell. Holland Park next door takes 27.

Twenty four days is a fast market by any measure. A home that is well presented and sensibly priced is meeting its buyer inside a month. A home still sitting at six weeks is telling you something, and the longer it sits the harder it becomes to recover.

91 houses changed hands over the year, roughly eight a month in a suburb of about 6,400 people. The pool of comparable sales is therefore small, and two or three results on your own street can move the number your home is worth.

Rates and prices

The market has cooled off.

Brisbane house values have now fallen six months in a row. They are down 1.5% in the last month and 4.9% across the quarter, and the city sits 5.4% below the peak it reached in May.

The annual figure still reads 5.3%, which is what most reporting quotes. In July the same measure read 14.3%. An appraisal from a year ago is now too low, and a number taken from the autumn peak is too high.

Houses here still sell quickly. The asking price simply has to be right.

Who lives here

Owners, not investors.

Just under two thirds of Holland Park West homes are lived in by the people who own them, 64.3% at the last census. The median rent is $750 a week, which on a $1,515,000 house is a gross yield of about 2.6%.

A yield that low tells you investors are not setting the price in Holland Park West. Families are, and they buy on the school catchment, the block, the floor plan and how the home shows on the morning of the open. Presentation counts for more here than it does in an investor market.
