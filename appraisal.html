What is your home worth?

An appraisal should leave you better informed whether or not you decide to sell. Roxanne prepares every one herself, backs it with the sales it rests on, and gives it to you in writing.

Why ask Roxanne

  • The sales behind the figure. The three homes most comparable to yours that have sold recently, and where yours sits against each of them
  • What is happening right now. What is selling in Holland Park West, what it is achieving, and how long it is taking to get there
  • What is worth spending money on. The repairs and improvements that return more than they cost, and the ones you can safely leave
  • When to sell. The timing that suits your circumstances, including waiting, if waiting will serve you better

Already spoken to an agent? Most owners are given a single number with nothing to measure it against. Roxanne is happy to provide a second, at no cost and with no obligation to list. If you have already signed an agency agreement, check its terms first, as most are exclusive for a set period.

Roxanne carries out every appraisal herself. She will walk through the home with you and tell you, plainly, what she believes it will sell for.

Not selling

What would it rent for?

Many of the people asking about value are not planning to sell at all. They are weighing up whether to keep the property, move out and let it, or work out whether the numbers stack up before buying somewhere else.

A rental appraisal answers that question, and it costs nothing.

  • A weekly rent range based on what comparable properties are actually leasing for, rather than what they are advertised at
  • How long it should take to lease, and the times of year that work for and against you
  • What tenants here look for, and the small improvements that lift the rent by more than they cost
  • The holding costs set alongside the rent, so you can see the real position rather than the gross figure
  • Sale and rental side by side, if you would like to compare keeping the property against selling it
  • Help finding a rental if you are selling first and need somewhere to live while you build or keep looking

Already renting it out?

It is worth reviewing the figure every year or two. Rents move, and a property that was well priced when the lease began can sit beneath the market for a long time without anyone noticing.
