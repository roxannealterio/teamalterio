Auction

Register to bid.

In Queensland only registered bidders can bid, and the auctioneer has to be satisfied of your identity before issuing your bidder number. Doing it here means you walk in, collect your number and go.

How auction day runs

No surprises on the day.

  • Register before you bid. Only registered bidders can bid. Register here and your number is waiting for you
  • Bidding for someone else? It must be disclosed before bidding starts and written authority is required. Send it through with this form
  • Buying in a company or trust? The name on the contract has to be right on the day. Send the details through now and the contract is prepared correctly
  • An auction contract is unconditional. No finance clause, no building and pest clause. Do that work beforehand
  • The deposit is payable on the fall of the hammer. Have it ready to transfer

Cannot be there? Phone and proxy bidding can usually be arranged, but must be set up in advance. Call 0419 382 733 to organise it.

General information only, not legal advice. Your solicitor should review the contract and the disclosure statement before you bid.
